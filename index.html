How It Works
STEP 01◎
Sense
Collect water quality, weather, environmental and community health data.
STEP 02⧖
Predict
Weighted risk engine combines signals into a transparent, explainable risk score.
STEP 03⚑
Alert
Early warnings generated with RISK → EXPLANATION → ACTION.
STEP 04⊸
Act
Response coordination ensures prediction leads to preventive action.
